## Features

- **Your app dock** — your PC's real apps with their real icons, laid out your way on your phone. Tap to open or bring to front.

- **PC controls** — volume, brightness, media keys, lock, shutdown, screenshots, and screen recording **with system sound**.

- **Now Playing** — see what's playing on the PC and pause or resume it from your phone, even in immersive mode.

- **Voice control** — in English or Roman Hindi: open or close any app or window by name ("close YouTube"), search the web, YouTube or Spotify ("search cars", "Avengers ka trailer"), set the volume, or run a Mode.

- **Touchpad & sign pad** — a smooth multi-touch touchpad with natural pointer acceleration, plus a drawing pad for quick signs and sketches.

- **Share files & clipboard** — send files and clipboard text between phone and PC in both directions, and grab the PC's latest screenshot or recording.

- **All your PC files** — browse Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos from the phone, sort them, or search any file by name in seconds.

- **Find My Phone** — ring your phone loudly from the PC, even when it's somewhere in the house on silent.

- **Modes** — one tap opens a whole set of apps together (for example, an Editing mode with your editor, browser and music).

- **Standard & Immersive** — a focused standard layout for everyday control, or a full-screen immersive dock.

- **Make it yours** — backgrounds and themes, your layout, your names on your apps.

## How it works

1. **Install the PC app** on your Windows PC from the download page.

2. **Install the Android app** on your phone from the same page.

3. **Pair once** — scan the QR code (or type the PIN) shown on your PC. After that, your phone connects on its own.

Everything runs right between your phone and your PC — no server in between, no sign-up, and nothing leaves your home. The two just have to be connected the same way: both on the same Wi-Fi, or your PC plugged in by cable while your phone is on Wi-Fi, or your phone's hotspot if you don't have Wi-Fi.

## Requirements

- A **Windows** PC
- An **Android** phone
- Both connected **the same way** — same Wi-Fi, or PC plugged in by cable while the phone is on Wi-Fi, or a phone hotspot

That's all — no account, no internet needed, nothing in between.

## Updates

Nexus keeps itself up to date. The PC app checks for new versions and offers them in-app, and the phone app lets you know when a PC update is ready — one tap and your PC updates itself. The phone's interface also refreshes itself from your PC, so small improvements arrive without re-downloading the app.

**Tip:** after an update finishes, close the app fully and open it again.

## Privacy

Nexus is **local by design**:

- It works only inside your own home — it has no cloud and no accounts.

- Pairing is protected by a PIN and a per-device token; only devices you pair can connect.

- Your apps, files and PC name are yours — Nexus shows each owner their own PC, nothing is shared or uploaded.
