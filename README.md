## What it does

- **Your app dock** — your PC's real apps with their real icons, laid out your way on your phone. Tap to open or bring to front.
- **PC controls** — volume, brightness, media keys, lock, shutdown, screenshots, and screen recording **with sound**.
- **Now Playing** — see what's playing on the PC and pause or resume it from your phone, even in immersive mode.
- **Voice control** — in English or Hinglish: open or close any app or window by name ("close YouTube"), search the web, YouTube or Spotify ("search cars", "Avengers ka trailer"), set the volume, or run a Mode.
- **Touchpad & sign pad** — a smooth multi-touch touchpad with natural pointer acceleration, plus a drawing pad for quick signs and sketches.
- **Share files & clipboard** — send files and clipboard text between phone and PC in both directions, and grab the PC's latest screenshot or recording.
- **All your PC files** — browse Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos from the phone, sort them, or search any file by name in seconds.
- **Find My Phone** — ring your phone from the PC, even when it's somewhere in the house on silent-ish volume.
- **Modes** — one tap opens a whole set of apps together (for example, an Editing mode with your editor, browser and music).
- **Always up to date** — the phone's interface refreshes itself from your PC, so improvements arrive without re-downloading the app.
- **Make it yours** — backgrounds and themes, your layout, your names on your apps.

## How it works

1. **Install the PC app** on your Windows PC from the download page.
2. **Install the Android app** on your phone from the same page.
3. **Pair once** — scan the QR code (or type the PIN) shown on your PC. After that, your phone connects on its own.

Everything runs on your home Wi-Fi, directly between your phone and your PC. There is no server in between, no sign-up, and nothing leaves your network.

## Privacy

Nexus is **local by design**:

- It works only on your own home network (LAN) — it has no cloud and no accounts.
- Pairing is protected by a PIN and a per-device token; only devices you pair can connect.
- Your apps, files and PC name are yours — Nexus shows each owner their own PC, nothing is shared or uploaded.
