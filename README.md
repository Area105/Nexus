# Nexus

**Your apps. Your controls.**

Nexus turns your phone into a beautiful dock for your Windows PC. Launch your apps, control volume and brightness, play and pause your media, send files, and run your PC from the sofa — no cables, no cloud.

🌐 **Website & downloads: https://area105.github.io/Nexus**

---

## What it does

- **Your app dock** — pin the PC apps you use most and open them from your phone; your whole app library is one swipe away
- **PC controls** — volume, brightness, media play & pause, screenshots, screen recording, lock — handled without getting up
- **Now playing** — see what's playing on your PC and control it from the dock
- **Share files** — send a photo or file straight to your PC, grab its latest screenshot, swap clipboard text both ways, or browse PC files and download them to your phone
- **Touchpad & keyboard** — use your phone as a wireless touchpad and keyboard for the PC, and draw a signature on its screen
- **Voice control** — tap Speak and say "open Premiere" or "set volume to 40"
- **Find my phone** — ring your phone loudly from the Nexus window on your PC, even on silent
- **Modes** — one tap opens a whole set of apps, each at its own volume
- **Make it yours** — five background themes, three glass styles, an immersive full-screen dock, app names on or off

## How it works

Nexus runs entirely on your own home Wi-Fi. A small host app lives on your Windows PC; the Android app pairs with it once using a PIN or QR code — after that, your phone and PC find each other automatically. Nothing leaves your network, and no account is needed.

## Get Nexus

1. On your PC, download and run **Nexus for Windows** from the [website](https://area105.github.io/Nexus)
2. On your phone, install the **Nexus Android app** from the same page
3. Open Nexus on the phone, type the PIN shown on your PC — or scan its QR code
