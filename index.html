How to use Nexus

Every feature, explained plainly. Tap a topic to open it.

Pair your phone and PC
Install Nexus on the PC and the app on your phone, and keep both on the same Wi-Fi. Open Nexus on the PC — its window shows a QR code and a 6-digit PIN. In the app, tap Scan the QR code (or type the PIN) and tap Connect. You pair once; after that the app finds your PC by itself.
Your app dock
Your PC's apps live one swipe away. Swipe up for the drawer and tap Apps to see the whole library; tap + on any app to pin it to your dock. Hold any icon to arrange: drag to move it, or tap it for more options. A pinned app glows — tap it in the list again to unpin.
PC controls
Drawer → Controls. Volume up, down and mute, screen brightness, play and pause, next and previous track, plus Lock and Sleep — your PC obeys from the sofa, no getting up.
Modes — a whole set of apps, one tap
A mode opens a set of apps together, each at its own volume. Make a Studio mode for your music apps, a Work mode for your office apps. Create one from the Modes card: name it, pick up to 12 apps, set a volume, save. After that, one tap sets the whole stage.
Voice control
Drawer → Voice, tap Speak and just say it: "open Premiere", "set volume to 40", "lock", "take a screenshot". Nexus listens, understands, and does it.
Touch & sign
Drawer → Touch & sign turns your phone into a wireless touchpad and keyboard for the PC — move the pointer, click, scroll and type from anywhere in the room. There is even a signing pad: draw your signature on the phone and it lands on the PC screen.
Capture — screenshots & screen recording
Drawer → Capture takes a screenshot of the PC or records its screen, for as long as you like. Everything is saved on the PC itself, in Pictures / Nexus.
Flick & clipboard
Drawer → Flick files. Send a photo or file from your phone straight to the PC (it lands in Pictures / Nexus / Inbox), pull the PC's latest screenshot down to the phone, and swap clipboard text both ways — copy on one device, paste on the other.
PC files — bring any file to your phone
Drawer → PC files. Browse your PC's folders — Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos and every drive — right from the phone. Tap any file and it downloads into your phone's Downloads. A song, a video, a document: if it is on the PC, it is one tap from your pocket.
Find my phone
Phone lost in the sofa? Open the Nexus window on your PC and press the ring button — your phone rings loudly until you find it, even on silent. On the phone, a full-screen ring page shows one clear button: tap it to stop the ringing.
Make it yours
Drawer → Customise. Three glass styles — Clear, Frosted, Smoked — four backgrounds (Aurora, Dusk, Ember, Midnight), 4, 6 or 8 icons per page, an immersive full-screen dock, app names on or off, and a Performance mode that keeps older phones fast. Everything answers instantly; nothing is buried.
Privacy & safety
Your phone and PC talk directly over your own Wi-Fi — no account, no cloud, nothing leaves your home. Turn on Auto-Lock and the PC locks itself when your phone walks away. The Recording Guard can warn you if an app on the PC starts recording. And pairing is protected by a fresh PIN every time the PC side restarts.
